About Me

Hi, I'm Anthony

I am a Lagos-based full-stack web developer with a B.Sc. in Computer Science. My background in self-taught technology combined with formal education gives me a practical approach to solving complex problems and building scalable systems.

Foundation & Journey

My interest in technology started early—driven by curiosity and hands-on problem-solving. From troubleshooting to understanding system architecture, I learned that technology is most valuable when it serves real people. This philosophy shapes how I approach development: every project is about impact, not just functionality.

Technical Expertise

I have built proficiency across the full software development stack:

  • Frontend: React, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
  • Backend: Node.js, Express, Python, RESTful APIs
  • Databases: MongoDB, PostgreSQL, MySQL
  • Tools: Git, Docker, AWS, Stripe integration
  • Design: Responsive design, UX optimization, performance tuning

Professional Approach

I believe in building with purpose. My work emphasizes:

  • Clean Code: Maintainable, well-documented solutions
  • User-Centric Design: Intuitive interfaces with measurable impact
  • Performance: Optimizing for speed and efficiency
  • Problem-Solving: Creative solutions within constraints
  • Collaboration: Clear communication with teams and clients

Beyond Development

I am passionate about empowering others through technology education. I have conducted workshops on web fundamentals, digital literacy, and best practices in online security. I believe developers have a responsibility to help others understand and benefit from technology, especially those with limited access to resources.
